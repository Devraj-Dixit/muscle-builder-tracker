Choose a session, enter the weight and reps you actually complete, then save it. Use a weight that lets you keep good form.

Training guidance

Start conservatively. Finish most sets with about 2–3 good reps still possible. When you can complete the top of the rep range with clean form, increase the load a little next time.

Warm up for 5–10 minutes and do lighter warm-up sets before heavy lifts. Stop if you feel sharp pain, dizziness, or unusual shortness of breath.
